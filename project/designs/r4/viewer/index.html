What changed

The decisions are in the geometry.

01 / STRUCTURE

Self-contained receiver

A reinforced core carries the mechanism through four mounting interfaces. No surrounding receiver rails are assumed in its load path.

02 / PAYLOAD INTERFACE

Located, supported, preloaded

Rectangular keys locate the adapter. Replaceable pads take up play. The 3.5 mm lug has wider sidewalls and supported roots.

03 / ACTUATION

A specified direct drive

The HS-65MG drives the keeper through a defined linkage. The payload remains supported by the keeper and receiver; there is no separate release sear.

04 / MANUFACTURE

Parts you can actually make

Print orientation, support access, dimensioned drawings, fit coupons, standard hardware and inspection criteria accompany the CAD.
