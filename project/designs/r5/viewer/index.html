Chosen for this box

Lower cost. Shorter drive.
One defined installation.

01 / ACTUATOR

A commodity metal-gear servo

A TowerPro MG90D from Adafruit is the selected budget part. It lies on its narrow side to fit the casing. Published stall torque is a sizing reference; loaded release still needs measurement.

Part & current price ↗
02 / MECHANISM

Only the travel we need

A short crank withdraws a guided keeper from the supported lug. The receiver carries payload loads. The servo drives the release movement.

03 / CASING

A shell around the skeleton

The casing encloses the mechanism. Separate physical bodies let you reveal its guides, lug support and mounting structure without pulling apart features that print together.

04 / INSTALLATION

Insert below. Fasten above.

A top access panel exposes the mounting screws. The cartridge slides into a defined bottom opening and fastens to an internal frame connected to the wing structure.
