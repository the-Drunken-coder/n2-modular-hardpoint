# N2 assumptions and unresolved decisions

This register separates nominal geometry, supplier statements, analysis choices and missing physical evidence. **No row represents a physically demonstrated N2 load capacity.**

| ID | Basis / assumption | Evidence class | Sensitivity and next resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| A01 | P14 is current; P6/P11 geometry and provenance are historical | Current CAD / revision record | Use current STEP and print STLs. The proximal roof/link/keeper changed in P14. A changed lug, roof, keeper, fillet or mount prevents direct transfer of a previous geometry screen. See [P14 record](../../docs/p14.md). |
| A02 | Nominal distal engagement is 0.6 mm; nominal open axial clearance is 0.7 mm | CAD geometry | High sensitivity to shrink, clearance, burrs, play, actuator stroke and fork lost motion. Measure closed and open states across retained play before loading. |
| A03 | Solenoid external envelope follows Adafruit 2776; hidden coil, wire routing and some small button/spring details are simplified | Supplier drawing plus modeling assumptions | Measure the purchased unit before printing a batch. Confirm clamp seating, rear-button capture and actual travel. The rendered internals are not a manufacturing drawing for the actuator. |
| A04 | PLA is homogenized as E = 2,200 MPa, ν = 0.35, density 1.24 g/cm³; tension/compression/maximum-shear thresholds 25/40/15 MPa. The P14 PAHT-CF estimate scales all strength thresholds by 47/31 | Analyst-selected surrogate, not anisotropic PAHT-CF FEA | Actual properties depend on lot, moisture, toolpaths, orientation, temperature and duration. Test PAHT-CF coupons and assemblies; neither the surrogate nor a coupon mean establishes a design allowable. |
| A05 | Parts behave as solid-equivalent material in the short static comparison | Analysis idealization | Sparse infill, voids, weak layer bonding and support damage are not represented. Inspect sliced thin walls and produced parts; record mass and process. |
| A06 | Contact locations and reactions can be prescribed for each retained-state case | Linear component-model assumption | Distal loss changes the raw threshold substantially. Real gaps, friction, local pressure and contact migration require measurement or an appropriate nonlinear contact model. |
| A07 | Current P14 receiver screens use continuous rigid backing. Ideal two-hole sensitivities exist for earlier geometry | Boundary-condition assumptions | P14 has no validated actual two-screw mounting model. Define backing, bolt/washer geometry, preload and local substrate strength, and test the representative installation. |
| A08 | Historical full-stroke hand calculation uses μ = 0.20 and idealized contact reactions | Assumed friction/contact model | Not measured PLA friction. Measure demand through the 0.6 mm support-loss and 2.3 mm unlock transitions, including skew and CG moment. |
| A09 | A supplier-listed 3 mm / 80 g-force actuator point represents available information | Supplier statement, incomplete curve | It is not a complete net-pull curve or a loaded-release capacity. Measure net force at position, voltage and temperature, stating how the return spring was counted. |
| A10 | 250 ms command, 10 s minimum off lockout and operator temperature gate are suitable initial bench settings | Project development choices | No thermal duty rating or separation success is established. Measure pulse/current/temperature/stroke and actual clearance; stop rather than lengthen pulses to mask binding. |
| A11 | Departure is externally driven in +Z | Mechanism definition | Zero, reversed or aerodynamic lift forces can leave the payload captured or touching the carrier after unlock. Establish real separation dynamics and corridor for each aircraft/payload. |
| A12 | P14 candidate is PAHT-CF with solid-equivalent toolpaths and the supplied print orientations | Proposed process, not validated | Follow [P14 print requirements](../../docs/print-requirements.md), inspect the 1.4 mm lug and bridge toolpaths, and record conditioning/profile/mass. Nominal infill/wall settings and geometric overhang checks do not prove print quality. |
| A13 | Host and payload mount hardware, backing and torque remain installation dependent | Open engineering decisions | Nominal Ø3.2 mm holes do not select a safe fastener or load path. Do not substitute the two M2 actuator-clamp screws for host fasteners. |
| A14 | Historical catalog prices and allowances indicate consumed-part budget | Recorded cost inputs dated 2026-10-26 | Not current price, availability, quote or checkout total. Obtain current procurement prices when purchasing and include all unpriced items. |
| A15 | There is no positive fixed-pod lock, installed temperature sensor, keeper sensor or departure sensor | Current design inventory | Do not infer those functions from spring return, controller state or LEDs. Adding them would require an explicit design revision. |
| A16 | 0.454 kg (1 lb) including departing adapter/hardware, 2 g and SF 2 produce 8.9 N design and 17.8 N sacrificial qualification targets | Provisional project requirements | These targets replace the superseded 4 lb case. They are not measured ratings. See [requirements](../../docs/requirements.md) and [test plan](../../docs/test-plan.md). |

The inspection, force-margin and timing gates in [verification.md](verification.md) are development criteria. The current [P14 test plan](../../docs/test-plan.md) defines the first bench campaign; the actual service wrench, environment, retention duration and reliability objective still need installation-specific definition before a working-load claim.
